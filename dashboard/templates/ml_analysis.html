Understanding Algae Bloom Risk

Algae blooms occur when microscopic plants grow out of control, depleting oxygen and producing toxins that are harmful to fish. Our Machine Learning model analyzes current and historical pond conditions to predict the likelihood of a bloom before it becomes visible.

Temperature & pH

Warm, alkaline water accelerates algae growth. High pH often indicates a current bloom in progress.

Nutrients (Ammonia & Nitrate)

High levels of waste products (Ammonia) and fertilizers (Nitrate) act as fuel for rapid algae reproduction.
