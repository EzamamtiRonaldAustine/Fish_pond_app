Our Story

Academic Genesis

Conceived as a comprehensive final year project by undergraduate students pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS) at the Department of Computing and Technology, Uganda Christian University (UCU), Mukono.

Research & Development

Developed with a focus on applying IoT and machine learning technologies to solve real-world challenges in local aquaculture, specifically addressing water quality monitoring and mortality rates.

Future Vision

Aspirations to scale this student innovation into a fully deployable commercial solution that empowers fish farmers across Uganda with affordable, data-driven decision-making tools.
