Our Story

Our team was brought together by a shared passion for aquaculture and a common mission: to solve the most critical challenges facing fish farmers today. After time spent in the field listening to local farmers, we saw firsthand how unpredictable water quality could devastate livelihoods.

This inspired our team to develop AquaGuardian—a solution built for farmers, by a team committed to their success. By combining rugged hardware with real-time analytics, we've turned those early field insights into a professional monitoring system that protects every pond. Today, our team remains dedicated to innovating alongside the farming community, ensuring a more secure and prosperous future for aquaculture.
