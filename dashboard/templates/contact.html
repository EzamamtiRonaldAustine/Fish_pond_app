Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about smart pond monitoring and fish farming best practices.

Recent research (such as studies in Uganda by Tumwesigye et al., 2022) shows that deviations in pH, Temperature, and Mineral levels directly cause stunted growth and low harvest weights. When fish are stressed by poor water quality, their immune systems weaken, making them vulnerable to lethal infections like Aeromonas (Fin Rot/Ulcers) or Columnaris. Our system tracks these metrics 24/7 so you can take action before your fish become symptomatic.

High Nitrogen (Nitrates/Nitrites) leads directly to Ammonia Poisoning and Brown Blood Disease, which prevents fish blood from carrying oxygen effectively, even if the water looks clear. Phosphorus spikes often come from overfeeding. By monitoring these specific nutrients, our system helps you optimize your feeding schedules—saving you money on feed—and alerts you exactly when a water flush is necessary to prevent mass mortality.

Rapid changes in temperature or water acidity can kill an entire stock in hours. Our hardware uses local edge-computing to automatically trigger water pumps the moment parameters hit critical thresholds. Research shows that such real-time automated IoT interventions consistently maintain fish survival rates above 90% in tropical environments by stabilizing the environment before human intervention is even possible.

High Turbidity (cloudiness) and Electrical Conductivity (EC) are major indicators of accumulated waste and mineral imbalance. High turbidity irritates fish gills and reduces growth rates. By tracking these metrics, you can identify exactly when your pond is becoming "overloaded," allowing you to manage water quality precisely rather than relying on expensive, scheduled water changes that might not be needed.
